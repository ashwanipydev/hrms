HRMS Project Design Report (PDR)

Client: BarodaSun Technologies Limited (BSTL)

Project: Supply, Implementation & Maintenance of Human Resource Management System

1. Executive Summary

This report details the technical and functional architecture for the BSTL HRMS. The system is designed to provide a unified platform for managing the entire employee lifecycle—from recruitment to separation—for over 300+ employees across regular and contractual categories.

2. Functional Modules

  • Core HR: Digital records, position tracking, and org hierarchy.
  • Attendance: Biometric integration (Thumb/IRIS), late marking, and timesheets.
  • Leave: Automated BSTL-specific rule engine and online approval workflow.
  • Payroll: Statutory deductions (PF/ESI), TDS, and automated salary processing.
  • Reimbursements: TA/DA claims with document verification.
  • ATS: Applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and onboarding.
